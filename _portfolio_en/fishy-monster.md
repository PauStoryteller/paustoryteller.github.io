## About this project

This was one of the first 3D sculpts I did on Zbrush as part of a digital sculpture subject at Escuela Internacional de Diseño. I regret not having the project anymore because I would have liked to make some retoplogy and leave this assets game-ready. It's a shame, but there are other interesting pieces with clean topology around here. Back then, this pieces helped me to get the hang of ZBrush as a tool and to develop a proper 3D sculpture pipeline.
